01
Prepare with intent

Go into the meeting with a hypothesis about the account, the buyer, and the decision you want to advance.

02
Run stronger discovery

Ask questions that uncover pain, business impact, urgency, stakeholders, and the buyer's current approach.

03
Present value, not just features

Connect what you show to the buyer's priorities instead of running the same generic demo for everyone.

04
Navigate objections with context

Explore hesitation and trade-offs before jumping to discounting or scripted rebuttals.

05
Close for a real commitment

Make the next step mutual, specific, and tied to the buyer's decision process.

DISCOVERY / UNCOVER URGENCY

When a prospect is interested, but not committed.

“What happens to the business if this problem stays exactly as it is for another six months?”

Why it helps: it moves the conversation from vague interest to the cost of doing nothing.

Less theory. More buyer momentum.

Don't just sound like a better salesperson.
Become a more useful one.

The goal isn't to force a close. It's to help a qualified buyer understand the problem, agree on value, and know what happens next.

Without a structure

Reactive sales conversations

  • The buyer dictates the agenda
  • Product tour before discovery
  • Unclear urgency and decision process
  • Follow-ups without commitment
A stronger approach

Buyer-centered deal conversations

  • Start with a shared purpose
  • Understand the business problem first
  • Link recommendations to measurable priorities
  • Agree on the buyer's next action
